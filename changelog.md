# SoundReach.me — Sitewide Changelog & Public Notice Log

All notable updates, platform enhancements, legal revisions, terms adjustments, and feature rollouts for [SoundReach.me](https://soundreach.me) are documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.0.0/), and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

This file serves as the **Public Notice Log** for all sitewide updates, providing users, partners, and regulators with an immutable, date-stamped record of changes.

---

## [1.2.0] - 2026-10-20

### Added
- **Public Legal Repository Notice Hub**: Integrated date-stamped public legal tracking for `terms.md`, `privacy.md`, and `changelog.md` directly within the repository.
- **Automated Payout Notifications**: Added multi-stage email and in-app triggers for creator video submission approval, milestone view reach, and balance withdrawal requests.
- **New Member Check-In Automated Sequence**: Integrated automated 8-day check-in cadence for onboarding artists and curators with active campaign tips.
- **Curator Performance Analytics**: Enhanced reporting dashboard with historical acceptance rates and listener engagement metrics.

### Changed
- **SEO & Marketing Showcase Integration**: Overhauled public documentation with complete marketing overview, conversion funnels, and feature matrices.
- **Clarified Buffer Settlement Timing**: Documented the 5 calendar day active tracking window and 5–7 business day pending clearing buffer in the Terms of Service.

### Security
- **Forensic Bot Detection**: Upgraded audio fingerprinting verification and automated milestone view velocity audits for creator TikTok submissions.
- **Tax Compliance Protocol**: Enforced automatic cashout locks pending Form W-9 / Form W-8BEN validation upon crossing the annual $600.00 USD IRS threshold.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-09-15

### Added
- **TikTok Creator Payout Calculator**: Interactive budget and payout tier estimator allowing artists and creators to project earnings across view milestones.
- **Mobile Progressive Web App (PWA)**: Added offline caching, standalone window launching, and native install banners for iOS and Android devices.
- **Available Sound Marketplace**: Real-time browsing gallery for verified TikTok creators to preview and claim active artist promotional sounds.

### Changed
- **Minimum Cashout Threshold**: Standardized the minimum wallet withdrawal threshold at $200.00 USD with transparent processor fee itemization.
- **Content Retention Policy**: Instituted the mandatory 45-day public video retention rule to prevent early post deletions.

### Fixed
- **API Latency Sync**: Resolved intermittent reporting lag between third-party video metrics counters and internal campaign databases.
