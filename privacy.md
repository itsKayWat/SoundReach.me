## 📌 Public Notice & Privacy Commitment

**SoundReach.me** ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy, personal information, and digital rights of our artists, playlist curators, content creators, and platform visitors.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, store, and safeguard your data when you visit [https://soundreach.me](https://soundreach.me), utilize our web and mobile applications, launch promotional campaigns, or receive creator earnings.

All updates to this Privacy Policy are committed transparently to this repository, providing a verifiable, date-stamped public record of our ongoing privacy commitments and compliance.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information in three categories to operate our promotion marketplace, verify campaigns, and process payouts:

### A. Information You Provide Directly
- **Account Registration**: Full name, email address, password hashes, and user role (Artist, Creator, Curator, or Sponsor).
- **Profile & Social Handles**: Public TikTok usernames, Spotify artist profile URLs, curator playlist URLs, social media portfolio links, and biographical details.
- **Payment & Tax Information**:
  - Payment routing details (PayPal email address or direct deposit information) for disbursement of creator and curator earnings.
  - Tax identification numbers (Social Security Number or Employer Identification Number via Form W-9 / Form W-8BEN) required by the IRS once cumulative annual payouts reach $600.00 USD.
- **Communications**: Inquiries, support tickets, and feedback submitted to our customer care team.

### B. Information Collected Automatically
- **Device & Usage Data**: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referrer URL, pages visited, time spent on pages, and system interaction events.
- **Session & PWA Storage**: Local storage tokens, authentication session cookies, and PWA cache elements required for persistent login and offline-capable dashboard navigation.

### C. Information Received from Third-Party APIs
- **Spotify Web API**: Public playlist tracklists, follower numbers, track metadata, and playlist add timestamps used to verify curator campaign placements.
- **TikTok API & Public Embeds**: Public video metrics (verified view counts, comment counts, share counts), audio track usage IDs, and video publication timestamps used to calculate view-tiered payouts.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data collected strictly for verified operational, promotional, and legal purposes:

1. **Marketplace Matching & Campaign Delivery**: Matching artist track submissions with appropriate genre-specific playlist curators and qualified TikTok creators.
2. **Performance Verification & Payout Calculation**: Analyzing automated social media API signals to verify that promotional audio is audible, retained for the mandatory 45-day window, and meets view tier milestones.
3. **Disbursements & Accounting**: Transferring creator balances ($200.00 minimum threshold) and reporting annual 1099-NEC earnings to the IRS.
4. **Anti-Fraud & Platform Security**: Forensic screening for artificial bot views, engagement pod activity, automated scripts, and unauthorized account access.
5. **Transactional Alerts**: Automated notifications regarding campaign approvals, submission feedback, milestone payouts, and system notices.

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosures

SoundReach.me does **NOT** sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party data brokers or marketing list aggregators. Information is shared only under strict operational conditions:

- **Between Marketplace Users**: When an artist launches a campaign, participating curators and creators receive track details, genre tags, and public artist profile links. Curators' feedback notes are delivered to the submitting artist.
- **Secure Payment Processors**: Financial transfers and payment processing are handled via PCI-DSS compliant banking partners and PayPal.
- **Infrastructure & Cloud Hosting**: Application data is securely hosted and cached via enterprise-grade cloud providers (such as Cloudflare) using modern encryption protocols (TLS 1.3 / AES-256).
- **Legal & Regulatory Obligations**: We may disclose information if required to comply with a valid court order, tax authority mandate (e.g. IRS), or law enforcement inquiry, or to protect the safety and legal rights of SoundReach.me users.

---

## 4. Data Security & Storage Integrity

- **Encryption in Transit & at Rest**: All network communications with SoundReach.me utilize secure HTTPS encryption (TLS 1.3). Sensitive tax documents and payment credentials are encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption.
- **Access Controls**: Access to internal administrative databases is restricted to authorized operations personnel using multi-factor authentication (MFA) and strict role-based access control (RBAC).
- **Retention Schedule**: Account data is retained for the active lifecycle of your account. Financial and payout records are preserved for seven (7) years in compliance with statutory federal accounting and tax obligations.

---

## 5. Your Privacy Rights & Controls

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the EU under GDPR, and California under CCPA/CPRA), you possess distinct privacy rights:

- **Right to Access & Portability**: You may request a copy of the personal data SoundReach.me holds concerning your account.
- **Right to Rectification**: You can update inaccurate profile or payment information directly via your dashboard settings or by contacting support.
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten")**: You may request account closure and deletion of non-statutory personal data, subject to the fulfillment of active campaigns and legal tax recordkeeping retention rules.
- **Right to Non-Discrimination**: We will never discriminate against you, adjust payout rates, or deny service for exercising your privacy rights.

To submit a data access or deletion request, email our compliance team at `support@soundreach.me` with the subject line *"Data Privacy Request"*.

## 6. Children's Online Privacy Protection

SoundReach.me is strictly intended for individuals 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from minors under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). Any account discovered to belong to an unauthorized minor will be promptly terminated and all associated personal data purged.
